PRIVACY & WEBSITE STATUS

Your information.
Clear expectations.

This notice describes the current preview website. A full privacy policy must be finalized before public launch or document uploads begin.

Account registration

Registration sends your first name, optional other names, email, trading market, and password to this website server. New users must confirm their email before starting an assessment. The server stores your profile, a salted password hash, simulated account state, trading history, and an audit record. The server also stores email confirmation status, hashed one-time verification tokens, and queued confirmation emails. Confirming an account email does not enable optional trade alerts. If you opt into notifications, it stores delivery preferences, push subscription endpoints, and queued notification messages. Resend processes email addresses and content for signup confirmations, requested password-recovery emails, password-changed security notices, and opted-in alert emails. Password-recovery tokens are stored as hashes; their one-time links remain private in queued mail until delivery, cancellation, expiry, or final failure. Recovery requests also store hashed email identifiers and request times to limit abuse. A completed password reset revokes all sessions and background push subscriptions while preserving assessment and agreement records. Browser push services process encrypted notifications and device endpoints. You can disable email alerts and remove this device’s push subscription in the dashboard. Logout removes push registration for this device; closing the page leaves background push active. Passwords are not stored as plain text. A session cookie keeps you logged in for up to seven days; logging out revokes the current session, and a password reset revokes every session. Practice reset archives the earlier attempt rather than deleting it. You can export your own records and request account closure from Account controls. Mutable operational data is removed when closure completes; the deletion receipt lists restricted immutable records that remain for retention review. This development notice must be completed with the legal entity, contact details, retention policy, and applicable rights before public launch.

Account security and sessions

Optional two-factor authentication stores an encrypted authenticator secret, hashed single-use recovery codes, verification counters, setup expiry, and short-lived hashed sign-in challenges. Setup keys and recovery codes are shown only during setup or code replacement. We do not send these secrets to an external QR-code service. Password recovery preserves two-factor protection. Session records include a private cookie-token hash, a separate public session identifier, activity and expiry times, and bounded browser metadata used to show approximate browser and device labels. These labels do not verify a device’s identity or location. You can review and revoke your own sessions from Security. Sessions last at most seven days and expire after 24 hours without session activity. Security changes are audited and may queue transactional email notices independently of optional trade-alert preferences. Do not share authenticator setup keys or recovery codes with support.

Academy administration and financial records

Authorized staff can review the records allowed by their assigned role. Admin access requires confirmed email and two-factor authentication. Sensitive changes and report exports require a recent password and second-factor confirmation. The academy stores internal review notes, sponsorship decisions, account restrictions, staff roles, and an append-only audit record with the acting account, time, reason, and limited action metadata. Approved traders can see the client-facing decision message. Sponsored-account details, externally verified profit statements, profit-sharing terms, and payout statuses are manual records entered by authorized staff; they do not connect to a prop firm or transfer money. Private evidence references and internal notes are restricted to authorized staff. You can see your own sponsored-account and payout records from the client dashboard. Authorized administrators can export bounded reports containing selected personal and assessment fields. The operator must define access review, retention, backup, and deletion procedures before launch.

Optional public leaderboard

Leaderboard participation is off by default. If you enable it and choose a public alias, eligible current contract signers appear publicly with that alias, qualifying assessment return, realized demo profit, trading days, completion date, and ranking. Email, legal name, private account IDs, signatures, and certificate documents are not included. You can change your alias or hide your entry from the leaderboard page. The ranking uses the assessment result linked to your signed contract; it is not a record of live sponsored-account performance.

Customer support conversations

Signed-in support messages, automated replies, human handoff requests, and staff replies are stored in the academy database and can be reviewed by authorized support staff. When generative AI is enabled, bounded chat history is sent to the AI provider to answer published company questions; account, trading, certificate, and agreement records are not attached. Provider response storage is disabled for these requests, and provider processing remains subject to its policies. Without AI configuration, the Academy guide answers from company information locally. You can request a human agent directly. Do not include passwords, API keys, payment details, or identity documents in chat.

Required trader agreement

After assessment completion, signing the company-provided agreement records your typed legal name, the profile name and email at signing, consent statement, signing date, assessment certificate, and exact contract version and document hash. Records stay in the private academy database after a practice reset. Earlier signed agreements remain available to you when a new version requires signing. These are electronic signature records; the website does not collect identity documents or independently verify your legal identity.

Account closure, exports, and payout requests

Account controls lets you download your own paginated personal-data export after confirming your password and, when enabled, a fresh second factor. Export pages are checked for changes so an incomplete or inconsistent download is not presented as complete. Account closure requires explicit confirmation. Staff assignments and unsettled sponsored-account or payout obligations keep a request pending until resolved. Completed closure removes the mutable profile, assessment state, authentication, notification delivery data, customer support conversations, and synchronized provider observations; the profile becomes a disabled tombstone. Immutable signed agreements, financial and review records, and restricted administrative records remain for retention review, as listed in your receipt. This is not a promise that all personal data has been erased. Authorized backups can contain earlier data until rotation removes them. A current deletion ledger is reapplied during supported recovery to prevent completed deletions from being undone. Payout requests store the requested amount, account, status history, and a hashed retry identifier; the site records external payment progress and does not send money.

Connected accounts and operational recovery

Authorized staff may connect an existing MetaApi account to your sponsored-account record. MetaApi processes the connected MetaTrader account and trading data under its own policies. The academy synchronizes account currency, balance, equity, and deal history for performance reports; the website does not collect your MetaTrader password or send trading instructions. Provider credentials remain on the server. History coverage, freshness, and unavailable observations are shown rather than replaced with invented data. The server also stores bounded error fingerprints, normalized routes, counts, and generic descriptions without request bodies, passwords, tokens, or raw error stacks. Scheduled local backups encrypt the database and private authentication/notification keys; each completed backup receives an isolated database and key recovery check. The operator must preserve the separate backup-encryption key and arrange durable storage and offsite copies. Recovery checks and fresh-directory recovery do not overwrite the running database; recovered accounts require password recovery, and older sessions, reset links, and recovery codes are invalidated.

Website requests and external fonts

Your browser requests page files from the website server. Infrastructure providers may process request information such as IP addresses and browser metadata under their own policies. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives font requests. External TradingView reference charts receive browser requests and the selected market symbol when loaded; they are governed by TradingView’s policies. The academy server requests public crypto price/candle data from Coinbase Exchange, intraday forex prices from Twelve Data when configured, and daily forex history from Frankfurter without forwarding your profile or session. Chart preferences are stored on your device for your signed-in user. This site does not include analytics or advertising trackers.

Before applications go live

We need to publish the responsible legal entity and contact details, purposes and legal basis for processing, information collected, service providers, retention periods, security measures, and applicable rights. The live application flow must provide an appropriate notice before collection.

Trading and identity records

Do not upload trading statements, identity documents, passwords, or payment information through this preview. Any future collection must use approved secure channels, limited reviewer access, and the selected prop firm’s verification process.
