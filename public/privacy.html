PRIVACY & WEBSITE STATUS

This notice describes the current preview website. A full privacy policy must be finalized before public launch or document uploads begin.

Account registration

Registration sends your first name, optional other names, email, trading market, and password to this website server. New users must confirm their email before starting an assessment. The server stores your profile, a salted password hash, simulated account state, trading history, and an audit record. The server also stores email confirmation status, hashed one-time verification tokens, and queued confirmation emails. Confirming an account email does not enable optional trade alerts. If you opt into notifications, it stores delivery preferences, push subscription endpoints, and queued notification messages. Resend processes email addresses and content for required signup confirmations and opted-in alert emails. Browser push services process encrypted notifications and device endpoints. You can disable email alerts and remove this device’s push subscription in the dashboard. Logout removes push registration for this device; closing the page leaves background push active. Passwords are not stored as plain text. A session cookie keeps you logged in for up to seven days; logging out revokes the current session. Practice reset archives the earlier attempt rather than deleting it. Data remains in the database until the operator deletes it. This development notice must be completed with the legal entity, contact details, retention policy, and applicable rights before public launch.

Customer support conversations

Signed-in support messages, automated replies, human handoff requests, and staff replies are stored in the academy database and can be reviewed by authorized support staff. When generative AI is enabled, bounded chat history is sent to the AI provider to answer published company questions; account, trading, certificate, and agreement records are not attached. Provider response storage is disabled for these requests, and provider processing remains subject to its policies. Without AI configuration, the Academy guide answers from company information locally. You can request a human agent directly. Do not include passwords, API keys, payment details, or identity documents in chat.

Required trader agreement

After assessment completion, signing the company-provided agreement records your typed legal name, the profile name and email at signing, consent statement, signing date, assessment certificate, and exact contract version and document hash. Records stay in the private academy database after a practice reset. Earlier signed agreements remain available to you when a new version requires signing. These are electronic signature records; the website does not collect identity documents or independently verify your legal identity.

Website requests and external fonts

Your browser requests page files from the website server. Infrastructure providers may process request information such as IP addresses and browser metadata under their own policies. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives font requests. External TradingView reference charts receive browser requests and the selected market symbol when loaded; they are governed by TradingView’s policies. The academy server requests public crypto price/candle data from Coinbase Exchange, intraday forex prices from Twelve Data when configured, and daily forex history from Frankfurter without forwarding your profile or session. Chart preferences are stored on your device for your signed-in user. This site does not include analytics or advertising trackers.

Before applications go live

We need to publish the responsible legal entity and contact details, purposes and legal basis for processing, information collected, service providers, retention periods, security measures, and applicable rights. The live application flow must provide an appropriate notice before collection.

Trading and identity records

Do not upload trading statements, identity documents, passwords, or payment information through this preview. Any future collection must use approved secure channels, limited reviewer access, and the selected prop firm’s verification process.
