ABOUT FUNDRAISER ACADEMY

Talent should have
a pathway to capital.

Some traders have a strategy and the discipline to execute it, but not the capital to scale. Fundraiser Academy is being built to address that gap through company-sponsored access to third-party prop accounts.

OUR MISSION

Back capable traders.
Build shared opportunity.

We aim to identify trading readiness, cover the cost of an approved prop account, and create a partnership where profits are shared and covered losses are borne by the company.

An academy built around sponsorship

Our focus is on traders with existing skills, rather than selling beginner courses or charging applicants for funding challenges. Qualification provides the foundation for deciding whom we can responsibly back.

The account itself comes from a third-party prop firm. Fundraiser Academy manages the sponsorship relationship; it does not replace the issuing firm's terms or promise that an account will generate a profit.

WHAT GUIDES US

01

Merit and discipline

Assess the quality of a trading process and respect for risk, not just a standout winning trade.

02

Clear expectations

Explain fees, selection, profit sharing, risk limits, and responsibilities before a trader enters a sponsorship agreement.

03

Shared accountability

Build a relationship with defined responsibilities for the company, the trader, and the account provider.
