THE FUNDING MODEL

You have the skill.
We help bridge the capital gap.

Fundraiser Academy is a trading sponsorship academy. We identify capable traders through qualification, pay for approved third-party prop accounts, and share eligible profits with the traders we back.

01

No participation fee

Traders do not pay Fundraiser Academy to enter qualification or receive a sponsored account. The company pays for the approved account.

02

Third-party accounts

Accounts come from selected reputable prop firms. Account type, available markets, eligibility, and trading conditions depend on the issuing firm.

03

A shared outcome

Eligible profits are split between the trader and Fundraiser Academy under a written agreement. The company bears covered trading losses.

WHO DOES WHAT

Clear roles.
Shared expectations.

We sponsor access to an account. The selected prop firm provides the account under its own rules; you bring the trading skill and discipline.

Fundraiser Academy
Organizes qualification, selects traders, pays approved account costs, manages the sponsorship relationship, and shares eligible payouts.
The trader
Provides accurate information, demonstrates trading ability, follows the agreement and firm rules, and manages trading decisions within the approved limits.
The prop firm
Defines its account rules, verifies eligibility, administers the account, and determines payout eligibility. Sponsorship arrangements must be permitted by the firm.

PROFITS AND LOSSES

Know the terms
before you trade.

We will publish the exact split, payout schedule, and covered-loss terms before you commit. No numerical split or account size is being advertised yet.

Profit sharing

The agreement will explain whether the split is calculated after prop-firm deductions, when payouts occur, and which eligibility conditions apply. Unrealized trading profits are not a guaranteed payout.

Company-covered losses

Our model places covered trading losses on the company. Drawdown limits, prohibited trading practices, and account closure rules still apply. Treatment of breaches and replacement accounts will be specified in the agreement.

BEFORE ENROLLMENT OPENS

Partner firms, account sizes, eligible regions, supported instruments, profit splits, and program capacity are pending. We do not currently claim an affiliation with any named prop firm.
