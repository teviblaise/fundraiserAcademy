YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Understand what our model offers, what still needs to be finalized, and what to expect before joining.

Is this a prop firm?

Fundraiser Academy acts as a sponsor for qualified traders. We pay for approved accounts from third-party prop firms; the selected firm issues and administers the account under its rules.

Do I pay anything to participate?

The model has no participation fee charged by Fundraiser Academy. The company pays for the approved sponsored account. Full cost responsibilities will be set out before enrollment.

Who is this for?

Traders who already have trading skills but lack capital to scale. The program is not currently positioned as a beginner trading course.

What are the simulator challenge rules?

A 10% realized-profit target, 10% total drawdown from the initial balance, and 5% daily drawdown from UTC day-start equity. Open losses count toward risk limits, and you must trade on at least 3 distinct UTC days and close every position to pass. A practice pass is not automatic funding approval.

What does qualification involve?

A review of trading ability, consistency, and risk management. Specific duration, scoring thresholds, instruments, and evaluation conditions are being finalized.

Which prop firms will provide accounts?

No partner list has been announced. Any selected firm must permit the proposed sponsorship and account-access arrangement. We do not claim a current affiliation with a named firm.

How are profits divided?

The profit split has not been finalized. The agreement will define shares, third-party deductions, payout eligibility, timing, and currency before you accept sponsorship.

What happens if I lose a trade?

The company bears covered trading losses under the written agreement. Prop-firm daily loss limits, drawdown limits, and other account rules still apply. A breach can cause an account to close.

Will you replace an account after a loss or breach?

No replacement policy has been announced. Any eligibility for a replacement account, and the treatment of breaches, must be included in the final terms.

Can I trade crypto and forex?

Supported instruments depend on the selected account provider. We will publish permitted markets and strategies for each program before participation.

Are profits or funding guaranteed?

No. Qualification, selection, account access, and payouts are subject to terms and eligibility. Trading may result in losses.

Can I apply now?

Not yet. You can register locally to try the simulated trading dashboard. Your registration and practice records are saved on the academy server. Formal application dates and the verified intake process will be announced.

Can people in any country join?

Eligible countries and age requirements have not been published. Company policy and the selected prop firm’s legal and identity-verification requirements will determine access.
