YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Understand what our model offers, what still needs to be finalized, and what to expect before joining.

Is this a prop firm?

Fundraiser Academy acts as a sponsor for qualified traders. We pay for approved accounts from third-party prop firms; the selected firm issues and administers the account under its rules.

Do I pay anything to participate?

The model has no participation fee charged by Fundraiser Academy. The company bears agreed sponsored evaluation costs and does not require reimbursement of ordinary evaluation failures or losses from permitted trading. Fraud, deliberate misconduct, and other material breaches remain subject to the agreement and applicable law.

Who is this for?

Traders who already have trading skills but lack capital to scale. The program is not currently positioned as a beginner trading course.

What are the simulator challenge rules?

A 10% realized-profit target, 10% total drawdown from the initial balance, and 5% daily drawdown from UTC day-start equity. Open losses count toward risk limits, and you must trade on at least 3 distinct UTC days and close every position to pass. A practice pass is not automatic funding approval.

What does qualification involve?

A review of trading ability, consistency, and risk management. Specific duration, scoring thresholds, instruments, and evaluation conditions are being finalized.

What happens after I pass the assessments?

You receive your practice completion certificate and move to the required contract step. Read and sign the current company-provided agreement, including progressive profit sharing and tier retention, before sponsorship review. A certificate alone does not approve funding. View your contract step.

Can I skip the contract?

No. A signed trader agreement is compulsory after the assessments and before sponsorship can proceed. Read the company-provided current agreement, and sign it with your full legal name after qualifying.

Which prop firms will provide accounts?

No partner list has been announced. Any selected firm must permit the proposed sponsorship and account-access arrangement. We do not claim a current affiliation with a named firm.

How are profits divided?

Newly admitted traders start at 50% trader / 50% company. At $10,000 in cumulative qualifying net payouts, the next eligible payout uses 60% / 40%; at $25,000, the next eligible payout uses 70% / 30%. Amounts are actually cleared company receipts after firm deductions and agreed transaction charges, before distribution. Earlier payouts are not recalculated. See all tiers and examples.

Does my earned tier reset with a new account?

No. During a continuous contractual relationship, your earned tier is retained across eligible sponsored accounts, additional evaluations, and approved prop-firm changes. Closing an account, an ordinary evaluation failure, or trading losses do not reset it. Qualifying net payouts across eligible accounts are aggregated. After termination, any tier on reinstatement or a new agreement must be expressly determined in writing before sponsorship starts.

When does payout processing start?

The company must calculate your entitlement and initiate payment within 3 business days after receiving cleared eligible funds from the prop firm. Operational deadlines use Monday–Friday in Africa/Lagos. The payout statement shows the qualifying net amount, applied tier, trader and company shares, and updated cumulative qualifying net payouts. The website records processing; it does not itself send money.

What happens if I lose a trade?

The company bears ordinary losses from permitted trading and agreed evaluation failures without requiring the trader to reimburse them. Prop-firm risk limits still apply. Fraud, deliberate misconduct, or material breaches are subject to the agreement and applicable law.

Will you replace an account after a loss or breach?

No replacement policy has been announced. Any eligibility for a replacement account, and the treatment of breaches, must be included in the final terms.

Can I trade crypto and forex?

Supported instruments depend on the selected account provider. We will publish permitted markets and strategies for each program before participation.

Are profits or funding guaranteed?

No. Qualification, selection, account access, and payouts are subject to terms and eligibility. Trading may result in losses.

Can I apply now?

Not yet. You can register to try the simulated trading dashboard. Your registration and practice records are saved on the academy server. Formal application dates and the verified intake process will be announced.

Can people in any country join?

Eligible countries and age requirements have not been published. Company policy and the selected prop firm’s legal and identity-verification requirements will determine access.
