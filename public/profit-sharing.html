THE THREE TIERS

A clear path from 50% to 70%.

BELOW $10,000

50%

Your starting share

50% to the Fundraiser · 50% to the company. Every newly admitted Fundraiser starts here.

$10,000 TO BELOW $25,000

60%

Your next milestone

60% to the Fundraiser · 40% to the company. Applies from the next eligible payout after reaching $10,000.

$25,000 AND ABOVE

70%

Your maximum standard tier

70% to the Fundraiser · 30% to the company. Applies from the next eligible payout after reaching $25,000, unless both parties agree otherwise in writing.

Thresholds use cumulative qualifying net payouts in USD across all eligible company-sponsored accounts, before distribution between you and the company.

WHAT COUNTS

Cleared payouts.
Real progress.

A qualifying net payout is an amount actually received and cleared by the company from the prop firm, attributable to your trading, after that firm’s deductions and any agreed third-party transaction charges, before sharing it with you. Unrealized account profits, deposits, and simulated results do not count toward a tier.

An upgrade starts with your next payout.

If your cumulative qualifying net payouts are $9,500 and the company receives another $1,000, that receipt uses your current 50% tier: $500 for you and $500 for the company. Your cumulative total becomes $10,500. The next eligible payout uses 60%.

At $24,500, a $1,000 receipt uses your current 60% tier: $600 for you and $400 for the company. Your total becomes $25,500, so the next eligible payout uses 70%. Previously distributed payouts are never recalculated.

TIER RETENTION

New account.
Same earned tier.

Your tier stays with you across eligible sponsored accounts, additional evaluations, new funded opportunities, and approved changes of prop firm throughout your continuous contractual relationship. A closed account, an evaluation failure, or trading losses alone do not reduce your tier or reset your qualifying total.

The company cannot unilaterally reduce an earned tier during an active contractual relationship. A change affecting that tier requires the written agreement of both parties.

PAYOUT PROCESSING

A statement for every
qualifying payout.

The company calculates your entitlement and initiates payment within 3 business days of receiving cleared funds from the prop firm. Operational deadlines use Monday–Friday business days in Africa/Lagos. Your payout statement identifies the amount received, applicable tier, your entitlement, the company’s entitlement, and the updated cumulative qualifying net payouts.

You can review recorded entitlements and payment progress in Account controls. The website records external payment activity; it does not transfer money.

The company covers agreed sponsorship costs.

The company bears agreed sponsored evaluation costs and does not require reimbursement of ordinary evaluation failures or trading losses arising from permitted activity. Fraud, deliberate misconduct, or other material breaches remain subject to applicable law and the agreement’s express remedies.

All arrangements must follow the relevant prop firm’s rules and required authorizations. The agreement does not permit prohibited third-party control, account sharing, trading, or payout arrangements.

Termination and a future return

Accrued and unpaid entitlements remain payable after termination, subject to the agreement. If you later return under a new agreement or reinstatement, the treatment of your previously earned tier must be expressly agreed in writing before new sponsorship begins.
