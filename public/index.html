A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What is Fundraiser Academy?

We back traders who already know how to trade but lack capital. After qualification, selected traders receive company-sponsored accounts from third-party prop firms. Profits are shared and the company bears covered trading losses.

Can I apply for funding right now?

Not yet. This website is a prelaunch preview. Participation is free; qualification criteria, account sizes, profit splits, and opening dates will be published before applications open.

Do I have to pay for a prop account?

No. Under our model, Fundraiser Academy pays for the sponsored account. The program is for traders with existing skills who pass qualification; sponsorship is subject to selection and availability.

Are trading profits guaranteed?

No. Trading can produce losses, and an account may be closed after a rule breach. The company bears covered trading losses under the agreement; that does not remove prop-firm risk limits or guarantee funding or payouts.

Do I need to sign a contract?

Yes. After passing the assessments, challengers must review and sign the trader agreement supplied by Fundraiser Academy before sponsorship can proceed. Until the company provides your agreement, this step remains pending.

How much of the profit do I keep?

The split has not been finalized. The written agreement will specify the trader and company shares, any third-party prop-firm deductions, payout timing, and eligibility before you join.
