SIMULATOR CHALLENGE RULES

10% profit target

Reach $1,000 realized profit on the $10,000 practice account over at least 3 distinct UTC trading days and close every position to pass the simulation.

10% total drawdown

Equity must stay above $9,000. This is a static limit from the starting balance, including open losses.

5% daily drawdown

Daily loss is capped at 5% of equity at the start of the UTC day ($500 on the first day). A breach closes positions and locks trading for that day.

THE PROPOSED PROCESS

From first review
to a funded opportunity.

The steps below describe our planned workflow. Applications are not open, and exact evaluation thresholds have not been finalized.

  1. 01

    Tell us about your trading

    When applications open, share your experience, market focus, strategy, and relevant trading history.

  2. 02

    Complete qualification

    Demonstrate your approach under published evaluation conditions. The duration, scoring, and loss limits will be announced.

  3. 03

    Review and sign your contract

    After passing the assessments, you must sign the trader agreement provided by Fundraiser Academy. Review the profit split, account rules, loss coverage, and payout terms before signing. This step remains pending until your agreement is supplied. View your contract step.

  4. 04

    Trade within the approved rules

    After your agreement is signed, selection and firm eligibility checks are complete, and the account is provided, apply your strategy within the approved risk and reporting rules.

GET PREPARED

A useful record
beats a big promise.

Start organizing evidence of your process. You do not need to send documents through this preview website.

  • A clear strategy summary
    Your instruments, entry and exit conditions, time frames, and risk per trade.
  • A trading history
    A consistent record showing trades, position sizes, drawdown, and results, with demo and live results identified accurately.
  • An honest review of setbacks
    What went wrong, how you responded, and what you changed.
  • An understanding of prop-firm rules
    Daily and total loss limits, restricted strategies, account access rules, and payout conditions.
