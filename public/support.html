How does the academy work?

Fundraiser Academy supports traders who can already trade but need capital to scale. Approved traders may receive a company-sponsored account from a third-party prop firm. The model has no participation fee; profit sharing and covered losses follow the company's written agreement and the prop firm's rules. Formal program terms are pending.

What are the assessment rules?

The simulated assessment starts with $10,000 in demo funds. Reach 10% realized profit without breaching 10% total drawdown or 5% daily drawdown. Trade on at least three distinct UTC trading days and close all positions to pass.

Why confirm my email?

Email confirmation is required before a new account can use the trading assessment. Open the link in your confirmation email, then refresh your client dashboard. Check spam, verify the address you registered with, and use “Resend confirmation email” if needed.

What comes after passing?

A passing challenger receives a certificate and can choose their name or nickname for it. The company-provided trader agreement must be signed before sponsorship can proceed. Passing and signing do not automatically approve funding.
