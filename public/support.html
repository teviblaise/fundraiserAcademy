How does the academy work?

Fundraiser Academy helps traders who already know how to trade but need capital to scale. Selected Fundraisers may receive a company-sponsored opportunity from an approved third-party prop firm. There is no participation fee. The company bears agreed evaluation costs and ordinary permitted trading losses under the written agreement; fraud, deliberate misconduct and material breaches remain subject to law and the agreement’s express remedies. Partner firms, funded sizes and eligibility details have not been announced.

How does profit sharing progress?

Newly admitted Fundraisers start at 50% to the Fundraiser and 50% to the company. Cumulative qualifying net payouts actually received and cleared by the company across eligible sponsored accounts unlock 60% / 40% at $10,000 and 70% / 30% at $25,000. The payout that reaches a threshold uses the earlier tier; the upgrade applies to the next eligible payout. Prior distributions are not recalculated. Qualifying receipts are after prop-firm deductions and agreed transaction charges, before the split.

Can an earned tier reset?

During a continuous contractual relationship, earned tiers carry across eligible sponsored accounts, further evaluations and approved prop-firm changes. Ordinary losses or account closure alone do not reduce the tier. An earned-tier amendment needs both parties’ written agreement. Accrued unpaid entitlements remain payable after termination under the agreement; reinstatement terms must be agreed in writing before new sponsorship. Prop-firm rules and required authorizations still apply, including restrictions on account sharing.

When are payouts processed?

The company must calculate your entitlement and initiate payment within three business days of receiving cleared eligible prop-firm funds. The calendar counts Monday–Friday in Africa/Lagos. Statements identify the amount received, applied tier, both shares and cumulative qualifying net payouts. Initiation is different from bank completion. The website records payout requests and external payment history; it does not itself transfer money.

What are the assessment rules?

The simulated assessment starts with $10,000 in demo funds. Reach 10% realized profit without breaching 10% total drawdown or 5% daily drawdown. Risk includes open-position profit and loss. Trade on at least three distinct UTC trading days and close all positions to pass; this is a minimum trading-day rule, not a three-day deadline or a 72-hour wait. After passing, choose your registered name or nickname for your certificate and sign the actual agreement with your full legal name before sponsorship review.

How do I confirm and protect my account?

New accounts must confirm their email before trading. Confirmation links work once and expire after 30 minutes; check spam and request another from your client dashboard after the cooldown. Password recovery links last 20 minutes. Security supports authenticator two-factor authentication, recovery codes and session management. Password recovery does not remove 2FA, and support cannot bypass email ownership or account security.

Where are reports and personal records?

Performance reports show recorded equity, monthly results, drawdown and consistency with source coverage. Staff can connect existing approved MetaTrader accounts through MetaApi for read-only performance synchronization. Account controls provide your own data export, closure requests and recorded payout history after security confirmation. Closure can remain pending while obligations are resolved, and restricted legal or financial records may remain. Account closure is separate from contractual termination.

Why are a trade or notification delayed?

Demo fills need fresh provider reference prices. Missing, stale, closed-market or quota-limited forex quotes pause fills; read the order ticket’s message. Background push requires HTTPS and browser permission, is limited to ten devices per account, and old routine alerts can expire after five minutes. Email delivery depends on the academy service and your inbox. Account-security emails take priority over routine alerts, but support cannot promise that a message has arrived.
